Changed in architecture.md (Idempotency and retries, The hand-off loop), contracts.md (`CommandSpec.needs_base`, `WriteCommon.idem`), DR-8 and DR-25, and new tests in `retries.rs` and `handoff_preflight.rs` (S4, S8, C4).

Changed in architecture.md (Processes and boundaries, C4 containers, The write path, Idempotency and retries, Live within 1 s, WS4), repo-layout.md (store area), DR-1 and DR-32, and new `stream.rs` tests (S8).
- correctness-3 (E3): the link snapshot is built inside the scan's transaction, keyed by its sorted member snapshot IDs and `packs_hash`, and named by `Scan.link` and `ReconcileOutcome.link`. Changed in architecture.md (Main flows, The fold, Hints and linkers, Store schema, The reconciler, R-1, WS4), contracts.md (Event model), DR-12 and DR-44.
